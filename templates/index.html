Step 2 — Enable Developer Options

  1. Open Settings on your phone.
  2. Scroll down and tap About Phone.
  3. Find Build Number and tap it 7 times rapidly.
  4. You'll see a message: "You are now a developer!"
  5. On some phones (Samsung), it's under Settings → Software information → Build number.
💡 Tip: You may need to enter your PIN/password when tapping Build Number.

Step 3 — Enable USB Debugging

  1. Open Settings → Developer Options (now visible from Step 2).
  2. Toggle USB Debugging to ON.
  3. Connect your phone to your computer with a USB cable.
  4. Change the USB connection mode to File Transfer (MTP) on your phone's notification pull-down.
  5. When prompted on the phone screen: tap "Allow USB debugging" and check "Always allow from this computer".
💡 Tip: If you don't see the USB debugging prompt, disconnect and reconnect the cable.

Step 4 — Connect Your Phone

Your phone should already be connected via USB. Click below to detect it.

Android 11+ Wireless Debugging

  1. Go to Settings → Developer Options → Wireless Debugging.
  2. Enable Wireless Debugging.
  3. Tap "Pair device with pairing code".
  4. Note the IP address, port, and 6-digit code shown on your phone.

Legacy WiFi ADB (Android 10 and older)

First connect via USB, then run adb tcpip 5555, then connect wirelessly.
